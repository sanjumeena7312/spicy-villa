Our Story

Spice Villa Café & Resto is built on the belief that great food shouldn't come
with pretence. From sizzling chaap and tandoori starters to wood-fired pizza
and comfort curries, we've grown into a neighbourhood favourite for pure
vegetarian food done right. Every dish is made fresh to order, from Kulhad chai to hand-tossed pizza.
Our kitchen crafts each order with the same care we started with.

Our Mission

To serve great food and warm hospitality without the fuss.

Our Vision

To be the neighbourhood's favourite spot for good, honest food.

Our Values

Quality, freshness, community and genuine care in everything we make.
